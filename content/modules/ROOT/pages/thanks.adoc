= Thank You
:navtitle: 6. Thank You

Thanks for taking the time to work through this lab.

You have stood up the Red Hat Offline Knowledge Portal on a RHEL host with
your own credentials, reached it through the bastion, searched the offline
knowledge base, and seen how Ansible and Podman Quadlet put it together. That
is the whole path you would follow on a customer's disconnected site — the
only things that change are their pull secret, their access key and their
network.

== Where to go from here

* Run it on your own RHEL host with the collection in the
  https://github.com/explicitworkload/rhokp[project repository^]. The README
  walks through a standalone deploy.
* Take it to a customer who cannot reach `access.redhat.com` — air-gapped
  sites, classified networks, manufacturing floors, ships.
* Remember the credentials model: the pull secret fetches the image, the
  access key unlocks the full content set, and the access key lives in a
  Podman secret rather than in the unit file.

[NOTE]
====
This environment is ephemeral. When your order expires, the node, the portal
and the Podman secret holding your access key all go away with it. Nothing you
pasted here outlives the lab.
====
