= Thank You
:navtitle: 7. Thank You

Thanks for taking the time to work through this lab. If anything about this
environment is unclear, or you hit something this guide did not cover, please
get in touch:

**John Goh** — mailto:jgoh@redhat.com[jgoh@redhat.com]

Feedback on the lab itself is just as welcome as questions about the portal.

Red Hat users who are not always connected to the internet might benefit from
having the Red Hat library of product and technical information available
offline — air-gapped sites, classified networks, manufacturing floors, ships,
or simply planned intervals of low or no bandwidth.

If you want to run it somewhere of your own, everything you need is in
xref:deploy.adoc[Deploy It Yourself].

[NOTE]
====
This environment is ephemeral. When your order expires, the node, the portal
and the Podman secret holding your access key all go away with it. Nothing you
pasted here outlives the lab.
====
