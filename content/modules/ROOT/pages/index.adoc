= Red Hat Offline Knowledge Portal
:navtitle: Overview

The Red Hat Offline Knowledge Portal (RHOKP) puts Red Hat's product
documentation, solutions and knowledge base on a machine with **no internet
connection**. Everything ships inside one container image, indexed by Solr and
served over HTTP.

This environment has it already running on a RHEL workload node, reachable
through your lab's public URL.

. xref:operating.adoc[Operate the service] -- logs, restarts, rotating the

[NOTE]
====
A cold start takes about 45 seconds while Solr builds its index. If the portal
does not answer immediately after provisioning, give it a moment before
assuming something is broken.
====
