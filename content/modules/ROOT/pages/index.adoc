= Red Hat Offline Knowledge Portal
:navtitle: Overview

The Red Hat Offline Knowledge Portal (RHOKP) puts Red Hat's product
documentation, solutions and knowledge base on a machine with **no internet
connection**. Everything ships inside one container image, indexed by Solr and
served over HTTP.

Ansible has already installed it on a RHEL workload node here. It is **not
running yet**: the portal needs a pull secret and an access key, and those are
yours, not the lab's. Your first task is to supply them.

[IMPORTANT]
====
Bring your own Red Hat account. You will need it for both credentials in
xref:credentials.adoc[step 1].
====

== What you will do

. xref:credentials.adoc[Get your credentials] — a pull secret and an RHOKP
  access key — and start the portal with them.
. xref:access.adoc[Open the portal] in a new browser tab and confirm it
  answers.
. xref:using.adoc[Search the offline knowledge base] the way you would use
  the Red Hat Customer Portal.
. xref:operating.adoc[Operate the service] -- logs, restarts, rotating the
  access key.
. xref:architecture.adoc[See how it was deployed] with Ansible and Podman
  Quadlet, so you can reproduce it on a customer's disconnected site.

[NOTE]
====
A cold start takes about 45 seconds while Solr builds its index, so give it a
moment after starting it before assuming something is broken.
====
